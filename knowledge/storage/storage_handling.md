> **DEMO / SYNTHETIC document.** Written for the Q-GenAI demo. It is not an official procedure of Blinkit, Zepto, Swiggy Instamart or any other company, and it has not been reviewed by a food-safety authority.

## Purpose
Keep products undamaged and correctly labelled while they are put away and stored on shelves, racks, chillers and freezers.

## Applicable Stage
STORAGE: put-away, shelf and rack storage, chiller and freezer storage, and stock rotation.

## Inspection Criteria
- Cartons must not be stacked above the marked maximum stacking height.
- Heavy items must be stored below light and fragile items.
- Storage locations must be dry, clean and free from sharp edges.

## Common Issues
- Crushed cartons caused by over-stacking.
- Dented cans from items falling off shelves.
- Labels lost through condensation in chillers.

## Required Checks
- Check stacking height and load limits during every put-away.
- Inspect shelves and bins for sharp edges or broken shelf lips.
- Check chiller and freezer areas for condensation affecting labels.

## Corrective Actions
- Re-stack over-stacked locations and move heavy items to lower shelves.
- Remove damaged units from the shelf and move them to the damaged-goods area.
- Repair or flag broken shelving before it is used again.

## Escalation Conditions
- Repeated damage in the same storage location.
- Leakage inside a chiller or freezer that may contaminate other products.

## Verification
- Inspect neighbouring units in the same storage location for similar damage.
- Re-check corrected locations at the next stock count.
