> **DEMO / SYNTHETIC document.** Written for the Q-GenAI demo. It is not an official procedure of Blinkit, Zepto, Swiggy Instamart or any other company, and it has not been reviewed by a food-safety authority.

## Purpose
Deliver orders to customers without damage and record customer-reported damage consistently.

## Applicable Stage
DELIVERY: transport by the delivery partner and handover to the customer.

## Inspection Criteria
- Orders must be carried upright in the delivery bag.
- Liquid items must not be carried underneath heavy items.

## Common Issues
- Items crushed by load shifting during the ride.
- Leaks caused by orders carried on their side.

## Required Checks
- Check that the delivery bag compartments keep orders upright.
- Record photos for any customer-reported damage.

## Corrective Actions
- Replace or refund damaged items according to the customer-resolution process.
- Report delivery bags that do not keep orders upright.

## Escalation Conditions
- Repeated damage reports for the same delivery partner or route.

## Verification
- Review customer damage reports weekly for repeated patterns.
