> **DEMO / SYNTHETIC document.** Written for the Q-GenAI demo. It is not an official procedure of Blinkit, Zepto, Swiggy Instamart or any other company, and it has not been reviewed by a food-safety authority.

## Purpose
Pack orders so that every item reaches the customer sealed, undamaged and protected, and catch packing defects before dispatch.

## Applicable Stage
PACKING: packing stations, bag and box selection, sealing of order bags, and liquid-item protection.

## Seal Inspection
- Inspect sealing equipment and verify seal integrity before releasing affected inventory.
- Check heat-sealer temperature and dwell-time settings at the start of every shift.
- Perform a squeeze test on liquid pouches after sealing; any wet seal or seepage is a leak.
- Remove and re-seal any order bag whose seal is incomplete or wrinkled.

## Liquid Item Protection
- Place liquid items upright in a separate leak-proof inner bag.
- Do not pack liquid items underneath heavy items.

## Inspection Criteria
- Order bags must be fully sealed with no gaps.
- Liquid items must be bagged separately and show no seepage.
- Fragile items must not be packed under heavy items.

## Common Issues
- Leaking pouches caused by weak seals or excessive pressure during packing.
- Torn bags from overfilling.
- Crushed items from incorrect packing order.

## Corrective Actions
- Stop using a sealer that produces incomplete seals until it has been adjusted.
- Re-pack any order that contains a leaking or damaged item, replacing the damaged unit.
- Reinspect affected units packed at the same station in the same shift.

## Escalation Conditions
- More than one leaking order from the same packing station in a shift.
- Any leak involving a chemical or cleaning product.

## Verification
- Re-inspect a sample of orders packed at the station after a corrective action.
- Record the sealer setting checks in the station log.
