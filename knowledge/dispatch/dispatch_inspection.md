> **DEMO / SYNTHETIC document.** Written for the Q-GenAI demo. It is not an official procedure of Blinkit, Zepto, Swiggy Instamart or any other company, and it has not been reviewed by a food-safety authority.

## Purpose
Check orders at handover to the delivery partner so that damaged or leaking orders do not leave the dark store.

## Applicable Stage
DISPATCH: staging area, order handover and loading into delivery bags or boxes.

## Inspection Criteria
- The order bag must be sealed, dry and undamaged at handover.
- Delivery bags must be clean, dry and have a working closure.

## Common Issues
- Order bags torn while waiting in the staging area.
- Leaks discovered at handover from items packed earlier.

## Required Checks
- Visually check every order bag for wetness, tears and an intact seal at handover.
- Check the delivery bag condition before loading.

## Corrective Actions
- Hold any wet or damaged order at dispatch and return it to packing for re-packing.
- Do not hand over an order with a visible leak.

## Escalation Conditions
- Leaking orders found at dispatch that passed packing inspection.

## Verification
- Confirm that held orders were re-packed and re-inspected before release.
