> **DEMO / SYNTHETIC document.** Written for the Q-GenAI demo. It is not an official procedure of Blinkit, Zepto, Swiggy Instamart or any other company, and it has not been reviewed by a food-safety authority.

## Purpose
Make sure packing materials (order bags, inner bags, boxes and seal film) are fit for use.

## Applicable Stage
Packing material receipt and use at packing stations.

## Material Checks
- Verify the condition of the packaging material lot in use, including seal film thickness and bag strength.
- Inspect a sample of bags from each new material lot for tears, weak seams and pinholes.
- Store packaging materials dry and away from heat sources.

## Common Issues
- Weak or thin seal film that does not seal reliably.
- Bags from a defective lot tearing under normal load.

## Corrective Actions
- Withdraw a packaging material lot from use when repeated seal or tear failures are linked to it.
- Record the material lot number with every reported packing failure.

## Verification
- Check other units packed with the same packaging material lot.
