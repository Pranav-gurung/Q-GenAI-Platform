> **DEMO / SYNTHETIC document.** Written for the Q-GenAI demo. It is not an official procedure of Blinkit, Zepto, Swiggy Instamart or any other company, and it has not been reviewed by a food-safety authority.

## Purpose
Ensure that inbound supplier deliveries are checked for damage, leakage and labelling problems before stock is accepted into the dark store, so that damaged goods are not put away or sold.

## Applicable Stage
RECEIVING: unloading of supplier vehicles, the inbound dock and goods-receipt scanning.

## Inspection Criteria
- Outer cartons must be intact, dry and not crushed at the corners.
- Primary packs (pouches, cans, jars, bottles) must show no leakage, dents or broken seals.
- Every unit must carry a readable product label with batch and expiry information.
- Chilled and frozen goods must arrive within their required temperature band.

## Common Issues
- Cartons crushed during supplier transport or unloading.
- Leaking pouches or bottles inside sealed outer cartons.
- Missing or unreadable labels on individual units.

## Required Checks
- Open a sample of outer cartons from each pallet and inspect the primary packs inside.
- Photograph any damaged unit at the inbound dock before moving it.
- Check that the received quantity matches the purchase order.

## Corrective Actions
- Quarantine damaged or leaking units at the inbound dock and do not put them away.
- Record the damage against the supplier delivery and raise a supplier claim.
- Reject the full delivery when damage affects a large share of the units.

## Escalation Conditions
- Leakage of any chilled, frozen or liquid food product.
- Repeated damage from the same supplier within a week.

## Verification
- Re-inspect quarantined units before any decision to return or dispose of them.
- Confirm that the supplier claim was raised and acknowledged.
