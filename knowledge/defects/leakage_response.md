> **DEMO / SYNTHETIC document.** Written for the Q-GenAI demo. It is not an official procedure of Blinkit, Zepto, Swiggy Instamart or any other company, and it has not been reviewed by a food-safety authority.

## Immediate Actions
- Isolate the leaking unit and any items it has touched.
- Clean the affected surface, tote or bag before reuse.
- Check other units of the same SKU and batch for leaks.
- Replace contaminated items in the order with undamaged stock.

## Food Safety
- Discard any food item that has been in contact with a leaking chemical or cleaning product.
- Discard opened or leaking food products; do not re-seal them.

## Investigation Checks
- Identify the first stage where the leak was observed and the last stage where the unit passed inspection.
- Check sealing equipment, packaging material and handling at the stage where the leak was first observed.

## Escalation Conditions
- Leakage of a chemical or cleaning product.
- Several leaking units of the same SKU or batch.

## Verification
- Re-inspect the remaining stock of the affected SKU and batch.
