> **DEMO / SYNTHETIC document.** Written for the Q-GenAI demo. It is not an official procedure of Blinkit, Zepto, Swiggy Instamart or any other company, and it has not been reviewed by a food-safety authority.

## Purpose
Handle any damaged product found at any stage consistently, so that it is not sold and the damage can be traced.

## Applicable Stage
All stages: receiving, storage, picking, packing, dispatch and delivery.

## Immediate Actions
- Remove the damaged unit from the sellable flow and place it in the damaged-goods area.
- Photograph the unit and record the stage where the damage was found.
- Check other units of the same SKU nearby for the same damage.

## Disposition
- Dented cans with a damaged seam or bulging must be disposed of, never sold.
- Products with an opened or broken seal must be disposed of.
- Cosmetic outer-carton damage may be released only after the primary packs are inspected.

## Escalation Conditions
- Damage to more than a few units of the same SKU on the same day.

## Verification
- Reconcile the damaged-goods area against recorded damage reports at the end of the day.
