> **DEMO / SYNTHETIC document.** Written for the Q-GenAI demo. It is not an official procedure of Blinkit, Zepto, Swiggy Instamart or any other company, and it has not been reviewed by a food-safety authority.

## Purpose
Make sure pickers select the correct item in good condition and place it in the picking tote without damaging it.

## Applicable Stage
PICKING: item selection from storage, scanning, and placing items into picking totes.

## Inspection Criteria
- The scanned barcode must match the order line.
- The picked unit must have no visible damage, leakage or missing label.
- Heavy items must be placed at the bottom of the tote.

## Common Issues
- Wrong item picked from a neighbouring bin.
- Fragile items crushed by heavy items in the same tote.
- Pouches punctured by sharp tote edges.

## Required Checks
- Scan every item and confirm the scan matches the order line.
- Inspect each unit for damage before placing it in the tote.
- Check totes for sharp edges or debris before use.

## Corrective Actions
- Replace any damaged unit with an undamaged unit from stock before the order moves on.
- Re-pick incorrect items and confirm the scan.
- Remove damaged totes from use.

## Escalation Conditions
- Repeated wrong-item picks from the same bin, which may indicate a bin-labelling problem.

## Verification
- Re-check the picked item against the order line before handover to packing.
